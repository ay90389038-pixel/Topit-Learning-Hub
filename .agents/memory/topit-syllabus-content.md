description: Content provenance rules for the TOPIT class 9–12 curriculum and question-practice demo.

TOPIT's topic outlines are broad, locally authored CBSE/NCERT-aligned demo maps. Do not describe them as complete or verified official syllabi; students must confirm class, subject combinations, and scope against the current official curriculum and their school.

Practice questions are original samples, not official exam questions. “PYQ-style” describes a practice format only; never present demo prompts, years, frequency tags, or sample-paper indexes as authentic past-paper records.

**Why:** Board requirements and subject combinations can change by academic year; TOPIT links to official source pages rather than importing their contents, and its topic map is broader than public board-paper coverage.

**How to apply:** Keep the demo/verification notices visible wherever syllabus or exam content is shown. Link to official sources when available, without implying that the local content is complete or current.
